## Addendum (2026-11-24): generic unit-pivot trace theorem

(mathlib job gate: exactly 3 axiom reports, allowed axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound,

## Addendum (2026-11-25): concrete component bindings

The generic row-trace theorem `rank_of_rowTrace`, a dense checker `rank_of_check` and a sparse checker
`rank_of_checkS` are built in the skeleton lane (CI-reported, not axiom-gated). They bind the
integral verifier's tables to the generic theorem for `D2` degrees 4-6, `D3` degrees 6-7 and `D4`
degrees 8-9, giving rank equal to the pivot count over every field. For `D2` degree 6 the build
succeeded but its axiom line was not captured. `D3` degrees 8 and 9 are not bound in Lean: the
kernel checks were terminated by the CI runner. The mathematics for those two is supported only by
the exact integer replay and rank computations over seven fields recorded in the local audit. Tables
are generated by the verifier; their construction from the complex is not proved here. See
`CI_RECEIPT_20261125.json`.
